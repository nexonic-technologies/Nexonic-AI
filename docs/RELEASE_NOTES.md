Early preview of Nexonic AI for Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or later, macOS 13 or newer).

## What's inside

- **Sharee and Nexa on video.** Both face you from the stage on front-facing video loops that fade softly into the dark.
- **A built-in brain that works offline.** Qwen3.5 2B ships inside the app and runs on the Mac with Metal. Nothing leaves your computer, and there is nothing else to install.
- **Your own cloud brain, if you want one.** OpenRouter (free models by default), OpenAI, Anthropic or DeepSeek, with the key kept in the macOS Keychain.
- **Conversation.** Sharee and Nexa answer in turn, or only the one you name, and the replies stream in as they are written.
- **Collective Intelligence core.** Six coloured nodes, with data flowing in and out along the fibres, all reacting to the cursor.
- **Today's Focus** and the permission panel with an emergency stop. Data is stored encrypted, with the key in the Keychain.

## Not yet in this preview

Agent memory, voice and lip sync, screen awareness and automation are still being built. The app says so where they would appear.

## Installing

1. Download the `.dmg` below and drag **Nexonic AI** into Applications. The download is about 1.3 GB because the brain is included.
2. This preview is not yet notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Nexonic AI. You only need to do this once.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lets you check the download: `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.
